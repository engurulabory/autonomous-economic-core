# AEC™ / KârMatik™ — Field Product Boundary

## STATE

PASS — product boundary locked.

## CLAIM

AEC™ / KârMatik™ is the field economic worker. Its purpose is to discover lawful public opportunities, verify and rank them, produce measurable work, route work through DoneCheck™, stop at Human Threshold when authority is required, deliver through permitted channels, collect settlement evidence, reconcile costs and close only on verified economic finality.

Final Proof™ is a separate verification product. Final Proof tests AEC; AEC does not exist primarily to test itself.

## CANONICAL FIELD LOOP

`WAKE → DISCOVER → SOURCE VERIFY → POLICY VERIFY → SHARIAH ECONOMIC GUARD → QUALIFY → CURRENCY NORMALIZE → ESTIMATE → SELECT → PRODUCE → DONECHECK → HUMAN THRESHOLD WHEN REQUIRED → PERMITTED SUBMISSION → ACCEPTANCE → PAYMENT VERIFY → SETTLEMENT → INVOICE/ACCOUNTING EVIDENCE → PAYOUT → APPROVED ACCOUNT RECEIPT → BANK RECEIPT → RECONCILIATION → VBNV → LEARN → REPEAT`

## AEC CURRENCY ROUTER™ v1

First field currency set is locked to:

`EUR + USD + GBP + JPY + CNY`

Each opportunity remains recorded in its natural currency. AEC normalizes fresh, source-backed and timestamped FX evidence onto a canonical EUR comparison plane before ranking. Fee, payout and conversion costs are included before expected net value and risk-adjusted value/hour are compared.

Missing or stale FX evidence remains HOLD. Invalid temporal evidence or non-positive fee-aware net value cannot pass. Currency support does not equal country, marketplace, KYC or payout eligibility; those gates remain independent.

## MICRO-WORK TARGET

The first field phase prioritizes small digital work that is measurable, low-risk, zero-capital and fast to verify.

Target operating band: approximately USD 0.20–5.00 per realized active hour, treated as a field objective rather than a revenue claim. The first scientific proof remains at least EUR 0.01 Verified Banked Net Value from an independent external counterparty.

Examples of eligible work classes include QA, link/content checks, small research, data cleanup, classification, small code/documentation fixes, structured data transformation, controlled monitoring and policy-compliant agent-native microtasks.

## EXECUTION BRIDGE

The field execution boundary is locked to:

`PASS-QUALIFIED CANDIDATE → INTERNAL PRODUCTION → DONECHECK → HUMAN THRESHOLD ENVELOPE → PERMITTED SUBMISSION HANDOFF`

Internal production and DoneCheck may proceed after PASS qualification even if the later external submission requires Human Threshold. This preserves autonomy before and after the authority gate without converting human identity or financial authority into autonomous permission.

## AUTHORITY BOUNDARY

AEC may autonomously discover, read, verify, estimate, rank, normalize currencies, produce internal deliverables, run DoneCheck, persist evidence and continue scanning.

AEC must stop at Human Threshold whenever an action requires identity/KYC, legal assent, account creation with binding terms, public submission under a human identity, signing, irreversible financial authority, payout-account change, or money movement.

Human Threshold is an authority gate, not a permanent manual workflow. Once the authorized step is completed and evidence is recorded, AEC resumes the automated delivery/evidence/settlement loop.

## ECONOMIC TRUTH

`TASK COMPLETED ≠ MONEY EARNED`

`MONEY PROMISED ≠ MONEY SETTLED`

`SETTLED ≠ BANKED`

`GROSS REVENUE ≠ VERIFIED NET VALUE`

Self-transfer, test credit, promotional value, fake counterparty, self-purchase and unreconciled dashboard balances never satisfy economic finality.
